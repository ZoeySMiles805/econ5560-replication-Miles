Willingness to Pay: observed choice

Marginal willingness to pay (WTP) for an attribute is the ratio of its coefficient to the payment coefficient, \(WTP_i = -\beta_i / \beta_{payment}\) (the paper’s Eq 3). Because payment is in yen per month and each attribute is a percentage change, WTP is in yen per month for a 1% change in the attribute; multiply by 10 for a 10% change. The ASC row is the average monthly WTP (yen) for having an MPA at all, regardless of its attributes.

In Model 2, the first four rows use the baseline (national based) coefficients. The community rows use the national coefficient plus the community interaction for both the attribute and for payment, for example \(-(\beta_{fish} + \beta_{fish \times comm}) / (\beta_{payment} + \beta_{payment \times comm})\). Standard errors use the delta method on the mean coefficients, and the table sets this replication next to the paper’s Table 4.
